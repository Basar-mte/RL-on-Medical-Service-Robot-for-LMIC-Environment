# Attribution: Gazebo Fuel models

The furniture, equipment and people models in this folder come from the Open Robotics collection on Gazebo Fuel (https://app.gazebosim.org). They are redistributed here under the licences given on their Gazebo Fuel pages. The authors are listed in each model's `model.config`:

* Wan Yi Seow (Open Robotics)
* Cole Biesemeyer
* Roselle Carmen

Models: BedsideTable, BedTable, CGMClassic, Chair, DoctorFemaleWalk, FemaleVisitorWalk, IVStand, MalePatientBed, MetalCabinet, Nurse, NurseDesk, NurseFemaleWalk, OpScrubs, PatientWalkingCane, PatientWheelChair, StorageRack, SurgicalTrolley, TrolleyBedPatient, XRayMachine.

They are used only for camera realism in the Gazebo worlds. The robot's LiDAR does not see them, except in the `furnished` world, where they act as unmapped obstacles.
