# Gazebo repeated campaign - success rates over pedestrian seeds

480 runs in 48 cells (tier x pedestrian level x policy x escape), seeds 1-10. Task: nurse desk (37, 15) -> emergency room (5, 7.5), `reached` = within 0.4 m inside 300 s simulation time. Each seed starts every walker at a different point of its loop (ped_routes.phases), so repeated runs meet different crowds. Success rates are frequencies over those runs with Wilson 95 % intervals; with ten runs per cell the intervals are wide (10 / 10 is [72, 100] %), and only non-overlapping intervals - or the Fisher tests below - support a comparison. Distributions are median [interquartile range].

## Data quality

- runs without a result: 0
- runs with a pose/logging gap > 1 s: 0
- timeouts recorded after more than 301 s: 0
- largest chassis tilt in any run: 1.0 deg (> 45 would be a knock-down)
